Appendix · Q&A
newdryve.

The hard questions.

Why won't TotalDrive just copy this?
They sell admin software to instructors who already have learners. Copying us means getting learners in each town, verifying instructors and charging late cancellations to cards, not adding a calendar.
Why won't AA or RED crush you?
Their instructors are franchisees who pay the brand a weekly fee. Newdryve is for the 86% who are independent.
Why will instructors pay £29/month?
Around 8,600 already pay for TotalDrive, for admin alone. We also bring learners and charge late cancellations: one new £40 lesson covers the month.
Why no commission?
A cut of every lesson is what instructors pay elsewhere (25% on EzLicence). A flat £29 is cheaper for anyone teaching more than a few lessons a month, and it's predictable revenue for us.
What stops them booking each other directly?
After the first booking they could go direct, but their diary, progress notes, cancellation charges, earnings, receipts and tax exports are all in Newdryve.
Is this venture scale?
About £42m a year in the UK at our pricing (37,441 independents × £1,116). Then Europe, and insurance and vehicle products for the same instructors.
