learner questions

Is Newdryve available in my area?

Newdryve is live in Norwich and the surrounding Norfolk area, including Thorpe St Andrew, Hellesdon, Sprowston, Costessey and Wymondham. Check your postcode and, if we’re not there yet, we’ll add you to the waitlist.

How do I book a lesson?

Open the app, browse instructors near you and pick a slot from their real availability. You need a valid card saved before you can book, but nothing is charged when you reserve a lesson.

Are the instructors qualified?

Every instructor’s current DVSA registration is checked before their profile goes live. You can also see their experience, specialisms and the areas they cover.

What happens if a card payment fails?

We try the card again automatically. If it still fails, you can pay in the app with Pay now. Until it’s paid, or your instructor settles it, you can’t book new lessons.
